## Search existing issues

Someone may have hit the same problem. Search the [issues](https://github.com/gajishere/surf-browser-releases/issues), including closed ones.

## Open an issue

If you cannot find an answer, [open an issue](https://github.com/gajishere/surf-browser-releases/issues/new/choose) and include your Surf version (type `ver` in Surf) and Windows version.

For security problems, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.
